RED RIVER DELTA v5h FINAL PIVOT RUN
Date: 2026-06-26

Purpose
-------
This is the final clean regional test after the national diverse-stream/kNN
selector was removed from the thesis headline. The goal is not to claim a
Vietnam-wide zero-observation PM2.5 map. The goal is to test whether a focused
Red River Delta model can estimate PM2.5 at a held-out regional station when
simultaneous PM2.5 observations from nearby regional anchor stations are
available.

Data and scope
--------------
- Source table: data/merged/unified_thesis.csv
- Thesis station scope: 40 KK stations with row-level PM2.5 QC
- Regional focus: 12 Red River Delta KK stations
- Rows after regional filter: 227,075 hourly observations
- PM2.5 QC mask removed 0 additional rows after dataset construction
- Regional station classes: 1 t1, 3 t2, 8 t3

External validation
-------------------
The final external validation uses the same delta_rfsi_wind model selected
from internal LOSO. Each external target uses satellite, meteorological and
temporal features extracted at its OWN coordinates (from the unified merged
table), plus RFSI computed at that location from the concurrent KK anchor
network. The target's PM2.5 is used only as the reference, never as input.
External targets are filtered with the same PM2.5 range, flatline, and
stuck-low quality mask used for the KK station rows. A target hour is scored
when it has both its own valid PM2.5 and its own meteorology.

External validation caveat
--------------------------
LCS and Embassy validation uses features at each target's own coordinates and
RFSI from the KK anchor network, so it directly measures regional transfer to a
new location with nearby active anchors. It is not yet a full operational grid
map: extending the same feature extraction to a dense grid of points (not only
the validation-station coordinates) should be listed as future work.

The LCS mean R2 is pulled down by a small number of sites with local baselines
that differ strongly from nearby anchors. The Hai Phong Bach Dang site has
PM2.5 mean 9.3 ug/m3 and hourly R2 = -4.452 because the model predicts it too
high. The Hung Yen Hong Cau site has PM2.5 mean 76.0 ug/m3 and hourly R2 =
-0.266 because the model predicts it too low. Do not call either site a data
outlier unless later evidence proves a data problem. Treat them as real
limitations of applying a regional anchor-based model to low-regime, high-regime,
or highly local sites that differ from nearby anchors.

National selector decision
--------------------------
The final clean national regime-routed stream test did not pass the agreed
threshold. Best clean national route:
- mean station R2 = 0.0786
- pooled R2 = 0.4215
- median station R2 = 0.0907
- positive stations = 25/40
- high-to-nonhigh flips = 3
- nonhigh-to-high flips = 3
- daily pooled R2 = 0.5291
- daily mean station R2 = 0.0358
- daily median station R2 = 0.0942
- daily positive stations = 25/40

This is below the target mean station R2 of 0.10. Therefore the thesis should
pivot away from a deployable national diverse-stream selector and focus on the
Red River Delta regional model as the clean, defensible main result.
