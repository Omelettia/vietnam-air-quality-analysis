# Thesis Package

PM2.5 prediction from satellite, weather, and nearby-monitor information.

This folder contains the final thesis manuscript, scripts, figures, and
canonical result files. The active scientific claim is a conditional Red River
Delta regional model using concurrent nearby-anchor PM2.5. Exploratory branches
from earlier development are kept outside this package in `archive/`.

## Final Thesis Story

The thesis now uses the national 40-station dataset to diagnose the problem,
then focuses the final deployable model on the Red River Delta:

1. A general national XGBoost model shows the difficulty of station-held-out
   spatial extrapolation.
2. Oracle tier/regime diagnostics show that baseline pollution level is central,
   but true tier is not deployable at an unseen station.
3. Within-station experiments show that temporal and meteorological information
   can predict fluctuations well once the station regime is known.
4. AOD and satellite products are useful but sparse and insufficient by
   themselves, especially for single-source/local pollution.
5. The Red River Delta is a defensible target region because the measured
   three-nearest-anchor geometry is tighter, station mean PM2.5 is higher, and
   concurrent regional anchor data are available for almost all target hours.

Deployment definition: the final model estimates PM2.5 at a Red River Delta
target location conditional on simultaneous PM2.5 observations from nearby
regional anchor stations. It is not a claim of zero-observation prediction
everywhere in Vietnam.

## Final Red River Delta Numbers

Best deployable regional model: `delta_rfsi_wind`.

Internal LOSO, 12 Red River Delta KK stations:

| Metric | Hourly | Daily |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pooled R² | 0.4233 | 0.4911 |
| Mean station R² | 0.2709 | 0.2835 |
| Median station R² | 0.3928 | 0.4778 |
| Positive-station rate | 83% | 83% |
| RMSE | 24.48 µg/m³ | 19.62 µg/m³ |

External validation:

| Target | Hourly | Daily |
|---|---:|---:|
| US Embassy Hanoi R² | 0.673 | 0.836 |
| LCS median R² | 0.307 | 0.355 |
| LCS positive-station rate | 73% | 61% |

The LCS mean R² is not the headline statistic because a small number of
sites with very different local baselines dominate the mean. Report median and
positive-station rate alongside the mean, and discuss the low-PM Hai Phong
case and the high-PM Hung Yen Hong Cau case as external failure modes unless
later evidence proves a data problem.

## National Diagnostic Boundary

The final national clean regime-routing probe reached only:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Hourly mean station R² | 0.0786 |
| Daily mean station R² | 0.0358 |
| Hourly pooled R² | 0.4215 |
| Daily pooled R² | 0.5291 |

This is useful as a negative result: national pooled scores can look acceptable
while station-wise generalization remains uneven. It supports the regional pivot.

## Manuscript Direction

The manuscript is organized around:

- why national zero-observation mapping is hard;
- why station baseline/regime matters;
- why AOD/satellite alone is insufficient;
- why nearby-anchor information and weather help;
- why the Red River Delta is the correct final scope;
- how a classic XGBoost regional model with RFSI and wind captures the useful
  information with a reproducible and interpretable pipeline.
